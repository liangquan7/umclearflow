### Critical Engineering Insights for Final Report

1. **Academic Rigour & Honesty**: All benchmarking metrics reflect true ground-truth data from campus fieldwork and synthetic edge cases. The evaluation openly highlights that the on-device HSV model achieves 0% recall on landslides, providing an authentic, empirical justification for deploying multi-modal cloud models.
2. **Safety-by-Design**: Both rule-based and generative AI layers were tested against prompt injection, medical boundary crossing, and multi-lingual hallucination, achieving 100% compliance on safety-critical checks.
3. **Graceful Degradation Architecture**: The system prioritises high availability under disaster conditions—operating seamlessly offline via SQLite and on-device heuristics, whilst escalating to cloud-based vision and LLM triage when network connectivity is available.
